# Product

## Register

product

## Users
DIY Homeowners who are looking to design, plan, and build their own custom decks. They need a tool that provides professional-grade structural validation while remaining accessible to non-engineers.

## Product Purpose
A 3D deck building software tool that enables users to visually design their decks, automatically calculates required structural footings and support elements based on physical constraints, and generates a bill of materials (BOM) and PDF blueprints for construction. Success is measured by the user's ability to confidently build a safe, code-compliant deck.

## Brand Personality
Expert, Confident, Professional. The tone should feel authoritative and trustworthy, reassuring the user that the structural math is sound.

## Anti-references
- Overly playful or cartoonish interfaces (avoids feeling like a toy).
- Overwhelming, dense CAD software (avoids intimidating non-professionals).

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Keep the interface strictly functional; the 3D visualizer is the hero.
- **Expert confidence**: Use typography and layout that exudes precision and structural integrity.
- **Guided safety**: Ensure error states and structural warnings are clear, educational, and not punitive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability in various lighting conditions (e.g., users might be outside planning).
- Clear, descriptive error messages for structural limits.
